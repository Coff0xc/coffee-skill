# Q2 Billing Operations Review

Audience: COO, VP Finance, Support leadership.

Known data:
- Invoice exceptions dropped from 418 in April to 271 in June.
- Manual review hours increased from 64 to 91 per week.
- 38% of exceptions are tax-code mismatches.
- 24% of exceptions are missing purchase-order references.
- 18% of exceptions are customer address normalization issues.
- The west region has the highest exception density.
- New validation rules catch issues earlier but create more review work.

Required sections:
- Start with the operating question, not a decorative title.
- Show where exception volume improved and where workload worsened.
- Explain the exception mix.
- Show regional concentration.
- Propose next operating moves.
- End with decisions needed from leadership.
